Country flags are bundled into `@helvetia-design/core` as inline SVGs, the same build-time approach as
[`ds-icon`](?path=/docs/components-data-display-icon--documentation)'s icons. There is no asset path
to resolve and nothing to configure in a consuming app's build — flags just render, in Angular, React,
and plain HTML alike. The trade-off is bundle size: every country ships in every consumer's bundle
regardless of whether the `countries` prop restricts the picker to a handful of entries. See
[ADR-0033](https://github.com/baloise/design-system/blob/main/docs/adr/0033-ds-phone-input-bundled-svg-flags.md)
for the full rationale.
