(available as `window.DesignSystem.snackbarController`, or via `dsSnackbarController` when importing
`@baloise/ds-core`). Calling `create()` mounts a `ds-snackbar` into a shared container, animates it in, and manages
auto-dismissal — click a trigger below to see it fly in.
